## 1:00–2:00 — Main Demo

### Screen Action:
1. On the **Message** tab of `/analyze`, click the preset button **"Guaranteed 30% Return Scam"** (or paste the text).
2. The message appears in the input area:
   *"Exclusive investment opportunity. Earn a guaranteed 30% monthly return. This opportunity is officially approved. Send ₹25,000 today to activate your account. Only 5 slots remain."*
3. Click **"Analyze Message"**.
4. The visual 4-stage scanner animates through:
   * *Preprocessing input...*
   * *Scanning linguistic patterns...*
   * *Cross-referencing fraud taxonomy...*
   * *Generating evidence report...*
5. The **Results Page (`/results`)** renders instantly:
   * Point cursor to the animated **Risk Meter**: **Score: 91 / 100 — HIGH RISK**.
   * Scroll down to **Detected Signals**:
     * **Guaranteed Return Claim** (*High Severity*)
     * **Upfront Payment Request** (*High Severity*)
     * **Urgency Pressure** (*High Severity*)
     * **Authority / Regulatory Claim** (*High Severity*)
     * **Scarcity Manipulation** (*Medium Severity*)
   * Hover over the **Evidence Snippets**: Highlight exact quoted phrases like *"guaranteed 30% monthly return"* and *"Only 5 slots remain"*.
   * Showcase the **Simple Explanation**: Read aloud the plain-language breakdown explaining why no legal entity guarantees market returns.
   * Point out the **Safe Next Steps**: Clear instructions on checking official SEBI registries and reporting cyber fraud to 1930.

### Spoken Script:
> "Let's test this guaranteed return message. When we click 'Analyze Message', Rakshak AI's hybrid risk engine immediately dissects the text.
>
> Within seconds, we receive a comprehensive, explainable safety report:
>
> First, a clear **Risk Score of 91 — High Risk**. 
>
> But more importantly, Rakshak AI breaks down the anatomy of the scam. It identifies five specific red flags: a guaranteed return promise, upfront payment demand, manufactured urgency, unverified authority claims, and artificial scarcity.
>
> Notice how every single signal quotes the exact evidence directly from the message. There is no guesswork. 
>
> And in our 'Simple Explanation' section, the investor gets plain, reassuring language: *No legitimate investment can guarantee specific returns.* It then outlines concrete safe actions to take immediately."

## 2:00–2:40 — Screenshot

### Screen Action:
1. Click **"Analyze Another"** or return to `/analyze`.
2. Click on the **"Screenshot / Image"** tab.
3. Drag and drop the test image `demo/sample_whatsapp_scam.png` (or `demo/sample_fake_brochure.png`).
4. Show the image preview.
5. Click **"Analyze Screenshot"**.
6. The backend extracts the text from the image and runs the risk engine, redirecting to the Results page showing extracted text, highlighted flags, and risk evaluation.

### Spoken Script:
> "Scammers don't just send text; they share fake certificates, forged broker statements, and WhatsApp chat screenshots.
>
> In the Screenshot tab, an investor can simply drop in a screenshot of a suspicious chat or social media flyer. 
>
> Rakshak AI extracts the textual content, processes the underlying claims, and surfaces the exact same explainable risk breakdown. Whether the trap arrives as plain text, an image, or a phishing link, Rakshak AI catches the warning signs."
